## Infiltrator
Roblox game collaboratively developed in four weeks using Roblox Studo, earning 2nd place in a game development competition.

## About the Project
Infiltrator was developed with the intent to make a co-op game that you could play with your friends. The game takes you through different rounds where your goal is to find take pictures of the "Infiltrator" who has three different stages. They are trying to blend in with your team and your team has to successfully complete the mission without being caught.
